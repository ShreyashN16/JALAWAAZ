The problem

When a handpump or tap complaint is marked fixed, usually nobody asks the person who reported it whether the water came back. Many people who depend on a village source cannot easily use an app or a web portal, but almost anyone can give a missed call and answer a question in their own language.

The number it is built to measure

False-closure rate = tickets reopened at least once ÷ tickets whose fix the caller answered, always shown with its n.

It is the district dashboard’s headline figure. We claim no value for it yet: no pilot with real callers has been run.

Code decides; models only hear, extract and speak

  • Priority comes from fixed rules, each with a stored reason: a contaminated drinking source, 100+ households without water for 2+ days, a repeat failure within 30 days, a reopened ticket.
  • SLAs of 4, 24 and 72 hours by priority, with automatic escalation when one is missed.
  • A database trigger rejects any illegal state change, so a claimed fix cannot become “verified” without the caller.
  • Verification calls: at most 3 attempts, never between 9 pm and 7 am.

Gnani models and what each one does

Prisma v3.0hears Speech to text. Each turn sends village names as key terms, the call’s topic, and the question just asked as context, so short answers such as “40” or “हो” are heard in context. A telephone mode tests 8 kHz phone-line audio.
Timbre v3.0speaks Text to speech, streamed so the voice starts on the first chunk. Numbers are spoken as words and ticket numbers digit by digit; mu-law audio for phone lines.
Evon v3.3extracts Turns the transcript into fields (village, source, fault, days, households) on a self-hosted server. A rule-based extractor takes over when it is not running.

Measured so far (13 November 2026)

  • 318 automated tests, including a browser test of the full report → fix → verify and reopen demo.
  • 12 of 15 held-out test calls (text) had village, source and fault all right, using the rule-based extractor.
  • Speech accuracy on recorded village audio: not measured yet.

Privacy and safety

AI disclosed in the first sentence of every call. Read-back before every ticket. No audio stored. Phone and device ids kept only as salted hashes; transcripts deleted after 90 days. One fixed health line and no diagnosis. The public dashboard view shows totals only.

Next

Native-speaker review of every sentence; a recorded speech benchmark including noisy 8 kHz audio; a small consented pilot with its n reported; a telephony provider for real missed calls.

In the demo the phone calls are simulated in a browser; the speech, language and ticket pipeline is real. SMS messages are logged, not sent. No complaint reaches any government system. Built with AI assistance during development; the product itself calls only Gnani models.
